One take, top to bottom. When you stop, it cuts the reading into the 27 clips the tour plays, one per stop. You check them here, redo any line on its own, and save them for the site.

BEFORE YOU START

  1. A quiet room. Phone about a hand's width from your mouth, and keep it there.
  2. Read the lines in order, at your natural pace. Sentences inside a line read normally.
  3. Between lines, a slow two-count. One... two. That gap is how it finds where each clip starts.
  4. Stumble? Keep going. You can redo any single line after.
  5. Saying it differently? Fine. Keep the lines in this order with the two-count between them. Tap CHANGE THE WORDS on a line, before or after, so the caption matches what you said.

Recorded in another app, or on a better mic? Bring the file in and it cuts that the same way.
