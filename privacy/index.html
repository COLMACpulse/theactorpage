YOU · PRIVACY

Your work stays with you

What the site does today. Last changed Oct 24, 2026.

THE SHORT VERSION

No accountNo sign-in, no tracking, no ads, no analytics, no cookies.
Kept on this deviceYour sides, scripts, takes, headshots, résumé, notes, contacts and voice live in this browser. We don’t have a copy.
Nothing leaves on its ownOnly when you tap to send, speak, search, check the news or put your page online. Each one is below.
You hold the backupOne file from On This Device. We never see it.

WHEN SOMETHING CAN LEAVE · ONLY WHEN YOU DO IT

When youWhat goesWho sees it
Send itA note, a pitch, a card, a page or a backup, from your own mail app or share sheet.Whoever you send it to.

A note to your reps or a pitch opens in your own mail app, or your share sheet, for you to send. A take, a page, a card or a backup you save is a file on your device, and you choose where it goes. SEND IT TO MYSELF on On This Device opens your share sheet with the backup file. Nothing goes out on its own, and nothing reports back who opened it.

Speak to a tool that typesWhat you say, while the mic is on. Eleven places; each says so where you use it.Google (in Chrome) or Apple (in Safari).

Eleven places can turn speech into words: Actor OS’s MATCHING WORDS, MEMORIZER’s SAY IT, NOTES, Paper Trail’s SAY IT, BREAKDOWN’s SAY IT (a note in your words), THEACTORPAGES’s voice and its GO, Project Hub’s Speak the audition, THE PLAN’s mic, Story Studio’s SAY IT (a note on a take), and WORDS on a video or voice note (FILM HUB and Paper Trail), which is off until you turn it on.

That uses the browser’s own speech service: Chrome sends what you say to Google, Safari to Apple. Actor OS’s LISTENING (hearing when your line ends) stays on the device, and it’s the one that’s on by default.

Put your page onlineNot switched on yet. When it is: that one page, with what you picked for it. TAKE IT DOWN deletes it.Anyone with the address. Our host, Cloudflare.

Not switched on yet. Hosting at yourname.theactorpage.com isn’t running, so nothing goes anywhere today. When it is: in Project Hub, PUT IT ONLINE sends that one page, with what you picked for it (headshots, videos, credits, your contact block if you leave it on), to our host on Cloudflare, at an address you choose. Anyone with the address can see it. Nothing else from your device goes. TAKE IT DOWN deletes it from the host.

The key that can change your page stays on your device, with a backup code you keep; the host keeps only a scrambled copy of each. Like any web host, Cloudflare sees that a page was asked for (an address and a time).

Put a video on your pageA YouTube or Vimeo link plays from that site.YouTube or Vimeo, for whoever watches.

A YouTube link plays from YouTube’s no-cookie player, a Vimeo link from Vimeo’s. When someone presses play, that site sees it, like any video it serves. A video you upload yourself plays from your page, not from them. Any other link is a plain link.

Check the newsNothing about you. One public file of headlines, the same for everyone.GitHub sees the file was asked for.

CHECK THE NEWS in Picture It goes online only when you tap it. A bar at the top says ONLINE while it does. It downloads one public file from GitHub, the same file for every phone: the California Film Commission’s tax-credit list and recent headlines, links, tags and short summaries from Deadline, Variety and The Hollywood Reporter.

Nothing about you is sent with it: no name, no wall, no offices. Which stories match the shows and films on your wall is worked out on your device. Like any download, GitHub sees that the file was asked for (an address and a time). The file is made once an hour by a feed reader that names itself, reads only those four sites, and follows their robots rules.

Search for your pressYour name and the words you picked, in a Google search.Google, like any search you type.

SEARCH THE NEWS, SEARCH THE WEB and SET UP A GOOGLE ALERT open Google in your browser with your name and the words you picked; Google sees that search, like any search you type. The press you keep stays on this device, and shows on your page only if you switch it on. The press kit is a file you save and send yourself.

Ask a friend to read inThe scene’s lines, inside the link you send.Anyone who has that link.

The link you send has the scene’s lines inside it, after the #, which browsers never send to a server. Anyone who has the link can read those lines, so send it only to someone you trust with the sides. Their recordings stay on their phone until they send you the file themselves.

Look up a credit in the HubThe title or IMDb ID, only if you added your own TMDB key.The Movie Database.

If you give Project Hub your own TMDB key, the IMDb ID or title you look up is sent to The Movie Database to fetch credits and key art. Without a key, nothing is sent.

Open a linkWherever the link goes.That site, like any link.

A reel link, a trade story, a casting site, or the SAG-AFTRA and talent-agent links on REPS open where they go, like any link. FOOTAGE trims your clip on this device and never downloads from a streaming site.

WHERE YOUR WORK IS KEPT

In this browser’s own storage on this device. Each tool keeps its part, and the tools read each other on the same device, so you add something once and every tool has it. Another phone or computer doesn’t see it.

The pages themselves come from GitHub Pages, which hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub sees that a page was asked for (an address and a time). It never sees what you put in. Their terms are in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

No cookies are set, and the pages load nothing from anyone else: the fonts and code are all served from this site.

CAMERA AND MIC

They only come on when you ask, and a bar at the top says so whenever they’re on, and whether they’re recording. They go off when you leave that step. What they record stays on the device until you send it.

OTHER PEOPLE’S DETAILS

Your reps, casting directors, cast and crew are kept the same way, on your device. CONTACTS keeps names, emails and phone numbers here. Their phone numbers and emails never go on the pages the site makes for you to send (the sheet for your reps, your film page).

KEEPING IT, AND LOSING IT
  • Back up. On This Device makes one file of everything, every tool, that you keep. We never see it. Restoring adds what’s missing and never removes.
  • The browser can clear it. Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings deletes it all. Safari on an iPhone can clear a site’s saved data after a stretch of not visiting; a site added to the Home Screen is kept. The site asks the browser to keep your work, and the browser decides.
  • Delete it yourself any time: remove it in the tool, or clear this site’s data in the browser.
