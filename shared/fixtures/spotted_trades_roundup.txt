EXCLUSIVE: Pilot orders are in. Here's what got picked up this week.

ABC has given a pilot order to 'Dry Creek', a one-hour procedural about a small-town sheriff's office. Wren Ashby is casting. Production begins in January in Atlanta.

Netflix is moving ahead with 'Salt Road', a limited series about cattle ranchers in 1880s Montana. Dana Pell and Ross Okafor are casting, with filming set for March in New Mexico.

Hal Brody will direct 'Northgate', an indie feature about a coffee shop on the night shift. Lena Marsh Casting is handling casting.
